🏔️ ALPINE HERITAGE

800-Fill Goose Down & Storm Defense

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, WinterCoatJet was established to bridge 800-fill European goose down insulation with 20,000mm hydrostatic nanotech stormproof membranes and Savile Row winter tailoring.

Core Outerwear Science

Sub-Zero Protection Meets Transatlantic Mobility

Mass-produced synthetic coats lose loft and trap perspiration during high-altitude travel. At WinterCoatJet, we source ethically certified 800-fill power European goose down, constructing sealed thermal baffle channels that lock in core body heat down to -30°C.

Each garment is tailored in our Soho atelier using 20,000mm hydrostatic head nanotech shells, creating stormproof parkas, trench coats, and wool overcoats that compress effortlessly into leather jet carryalls.

1. 800-Fill Down

Maximum thermal loft and warmth-to-weight ratio.

2. 20,000mm Shell

Hydrophobic nanotech protection against sleet and snow.

3. -30°C Rating

Sub-zero arctic thermal protection in polar blizzards.

4. Savile Tailoring

Canvas chest piece and double-breasted elegance.
